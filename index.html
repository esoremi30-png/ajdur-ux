Key Inputs from Prof. Kewunla & Ajdur Team

The 132-page strategic brief and audio conversation with Prof. Kewunla resolved all 5 major flow decisions and introduced AdMob rewarded ads as a core monetisation model. Below are the critical strategic inputs that now shape every design decision.

✅ Confirmed — Core Mission
Transform from "Basic Utility App" to "Premium Spiritual Intelligence Platform"
The brief is explicit: the current app is functional but does not communicate elite value. Users must feel trust, mystery, authority, premium quality, and transformation within 3 seconds of opening the app. Everything else is secondary to this.
✅ Confirmed — Conversion Model
4-Step Funnel: Onboarding → Free Hook → Premium Unlock → Consultation
Users are guided from curiosity → trust → small action → emotional investment → premium purchase. The Self Discovery home reading gives a meaningful free result, then every result screen must route toward "Speak to Founder/Owner" Consultation.
⚡ New — AdMob Rewarded Video Ads
Hybrid Revenue: Direct payment + Rewarded ad video as alternative access
Users who cannot pay watch a rewarded video to unlock Advance Insights features. This must feel like "Earn Access" not "Forced Ad." Language: "Watch & Unlock" — never "Watch Ad." Premium users see no forced ads. Never show ads on consultation, payment, or result screens.
✅ Confirmed — Visual Direction Preference
Deep Midnight Blue + Elegant Gold + Ivory — "Apple meets Spiritual Intelligence"
Prof. Kewunla's brief specifies a dark luxury spiritual aesthetic. We now keep that direction as Option A, add a premium lavender-led Option B from the reference palette, and introduce a cooler premium blue-slate Option C. The final choice is yours — see Design section.
✅ Confirmed — Service Names Updated
All 12 screen labels, titles, and CTAs upgraded with conversion copy from brief
"Get Started" → "Reveal My Blueprint" · "Consultation" → "Speak to Founder/Owner" · "Standard" → "Clarity Session" · "Platinum/Diamond" → "Deep Destiny Consultation" with "MOST RECOMMENDED" badge · "Request Coupon" → "Unlock Discount Access"
⚠ Still Open — 3 Business Items
These 3 items must be confirmed before development can finalise those screens
1. Consultation pricing — exact prices for Clarity Session and Deep Destiny Consultation needed.
2. Coupon types — the specific discount percentages and credit amounts not yet defined.
3. Store timeline — Books, Incenses, and Healing Products are confirmed; when do they launch?

Deliverable 02

Updated Redesign Priorities

These are my suggestions to the Ajdur App Team. They are subject to the team's final decision. Each priority area drives a specific business outcome.

These are design and development mandates — not optional enhancements.

01 · Onboarding
Psychologically Compelling First Impression
Shift from informative to irresistible. Current onboarding feels nice — elite onboarding makes users feel "I NEED to continue." Use stronger emotional headlines ("Why Does Life Keep Repeating the Same Lessons?"), lighter premium aura visuals, a trust signal ("Trusted by Thousands Seeking Clarity"), a Skip option, and a powerful CTA: Reveal My Blueprint.
02 · Home Dashboard
Guided Welcome Dashboard, Not a Menu
Rebuild as a personalised entry point: daily inspirational message, primary action strip with two CTAs (Reveal Blueprint / Book Consultation), a featured insight card, a consultation upsell banner, and a clear "what to do next" signal. Users must feel directed — not overwhelmed by choices.
03 · Discover Hidden Patterns
Discovery Hub, Not a Utility Grid
Replace the equal-weight grey tile grid with an addictive discovery experience. Add a hero section ("What Is Affecting Your Life Right Now?"), a featured premium insight spotlight, categorised cards with emotional hook copy, free/unlock/coming-soon status indicators, and a direct pathway to Consultation after each result.
04 · Speak to Founder/Owner
Premium Conversion Page, Not a Service Listing
Transform the Consultation page into the sales engine of the app. Outcome-led tier cards (what the user gets, not what the service is), comparison table, trust builder block ("Why Users Trust Ajdur"), testimonials section, "Most Recommended" badge on Deep Destiny Consultation, and soft urgency ("Limited weekly slots available").
05 · Unlock Discount Access
Accessibility Pathway, Not a Coupon Form
Reposition the entire page around human dignity and access. Copy: "Everyone Deserves Clarity." Add a parallel "Prefer Free Access?" block that routes users into rewarded ad unlocks without feeling cheap or transactional. Two CTAs: "Request Access Support" (coupon) and "Earn Your Reading" (ad path).
06 · Daily Wisdom Center
Authority Hub, Not a Login Dead-End
Separate the Spiritual Academy class portal (login link) from daily wisdom content. Build a Daily Wisdom feed with rotating spiritual teachings, numerology lessons, dream tips, and daily oracle messages. This drives daily return, ad revenue, and trust — making Prof. Kewunla's expertise visible to users who haven't yet booked a Consultation.
Deliverable 03

All Recommended User Flows

Every major user journey mapped from entry point to value moment. These are the paths the app must support — each one contributes to either direct revenue, ad revenue, or retention.

Flow 1 — First-Time User Onboarding
App install → Emotional hook → Home dashboard → First free reading → Consultation upsell
Flow 2 — Daily Returning User
App re-open → Daily wisdom → Insight or Consultation → Ad revenue loop
Flow 3 — Consultation Booking
Menu → Speak to Founder/Owner → Tier selection → Optional coupon → Confirm → Booked
Flow 4 — Discover Hidden Patterns (Advance Insights)
Grid → Tap card → Access choice (Pay or Watch Ad) → Result → Consultation upsell
Flow 5 — Rewarded Ad Access (AdMob)
User chooses "Watch & Unlock" → Watches full ad → Starter reading unlocked → Post-result upsell
Flow 6 — Daily Wisdom Center (Education)
Menu → Daily Wisdom → Read content → Optional premium lesson (ad) → Class login (optional)
Flow 7 — Unlock Discount Access (Coupon)
Two parallel paths: coupon request form OR free access via rewarded ad
Flow 8 — Post-Insight Consultation Upsell (Critical Revenue Path)
After EVERY insight result — the primary conversion path from free user to paying client
